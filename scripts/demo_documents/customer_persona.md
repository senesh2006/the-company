# Ideal Customer Profile (ICP) & Buyer Personas

## Primary Persona: "Strategic Samantha" — VP of Finance & Operations

### Demographics & Profile
- **Role**: VP of Finance, Head of Finance, or Director of RevOps
- **Company Profile**: B2B AI / SaaS, 30–150 employees, $3M–$15M ARR, Series A or B funded
- **Tech Stack**: QuickBooks Online / NetSuite, Stripe / Chargebee, Salesforce / HubSpot, Google Workspace, Slack

### Goals & Motivations
1. **Reduce Month-End Close Time**: Compress the monthly closing cycle from 15 days down to under 2 days.
2. **Eliminate Billing Leaks**: Identify unbilled usage, mismatched contract renewals, and payment gateway charge failures automatically.
3. **Deliver Board-Ready Insights**: Provide real-time cash flow runway calculations and ARR cohort waterfalls to the CEO and Board of Directors.

### Primary Pain Points & Frustrations
- Spending 20+ hours per week chasing sales reps for signed SOWs and PO numbers.
- Constant fear of broken Excel formulas corrupting historical financial statements.
- Lack of engineering resources to build custom integrations between billing systems and ERPs.

### Why She Buys Aperture Analytics
- **Fast Time-to-Value**: Connects to existing Google Sheets and billing tools in under 5 minutes with zero developer overhead.
- **Autonomous Peace of Mind**: The AI Controller autonomously audits every debit and credit entry and maintains verifiable trial balance equilibrium.

## Secondary Persona: "Builder Ben" — Technical Founder & CEO

### Demographics & Profile
- **Role**: Founder, CEO, or CTO
- **Company Profile**: Early-stage B2B SaaS, 5–25 employees, $500k–$3M ARR, Seed funded
- **Tech Stack**: Next.js, FastAPI, Supabase, Stripe, GitHub, Google Sheets

### Goals & Motivations
1. Keep burn lean while maintaining institutional-grade corporate governance.
2. Delegate operational busywork (invoicing, vendor management, contract reviews) to AI agents without hiring expensive back-office staff.
3. Keep focus 100% on product development and customer acquisition.

### Key Trigger Events
- Closing a seed round and needing to report professional financials to investors.
- Hitting $25k+ MRR where manual invoice generation begins breaking down.
