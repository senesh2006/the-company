# Aperture Analytics — Internal HR Policies & Operating Guidelines

**Document Category**: Company Policy & Governance  
**Owner**: Alex Morgan (Founder & CEO)  
**Effective Date**: January 2026 (Updated August 2026)

---

## 1. Work Environment & Remote Policy
- **Remote-First Organization**: Aperture Analytics operates as a distributed, remote-first team. Core collaboration hours are 10:00 AM – 3:00 PM EST to ensure synchronous overlap.
- **Home Office Hardware Stipend**: Every full-time team member receives a **$1,500 annual stipend** for workstation equipment, monitors, and ergonomic setups.

---

## 2. Expense & Financial Purchasing Policy
- **Automated Approval Threshold**: Operational expenses, SaaS tool subscriptions, and software licenses under **$500.00 USD** are auto-approved by the Finance Controller agent.
- **Executive Review Threshold**: Purchases, vendor contracts, or hardware requests exceeding **$500.00 USD** require explicit founder/executive approval.
- **Reimbursement Turnaround**: Expense reports submitted via the portal are processed and reimbursed within 3 business days.

---

## 3. Paid Time Off (PTO) & Leave Policy
- **Flexible PTO Policy**: Team members are encouraged to take a minimum of **15 days of PTO** per year, plus company-observed national holidays.
- **Health & Wellness Days**: 5 dedicated wellness/sick days per year available immediately upon hire without advance notice required.

---

## 4. Security & Data Protection Hygiene
- **Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA)**: Mandatory 2FA/MFA enforced across all corporate accounts (Google Workspace, GitHub, Supabase, Vercel, Stripe).
- **Password Management**: All credentials must be stored in the official enterprise password manager; plain-text passwords in chats or code repositories are strictly prohibited.
