# Aperture Analytics — Executive Pitch & Company Overview

## Mission & Vision
**Aperture Analytics** builds autonomous revenue intelligence and automated financial operations software for high-growth B2B SaaS organizations. Our platform bridges the gap between disparate CRM pipelines, billing engines, and general ledgers to provide real-time variance detection, predictive churn modeling, and automated reconciliation.

---

## The Problem
Mid-market B2B software companies with $2M–$50M in ARR suffer from fragmented financial and revenue visibility:
1. **Siloed Systems**: CRM data (Salesforce, HubSpot) does not match billing data (Stripe, Chargebee) or ERP records (NetSuite, QuickBooks).
2. **Delayed Month-End Close**: Finance teams spend 12–18 business days manually reconciling revenue recognition and deferred revenue schedules.
3. **Reactive Churn Detection**: Usage drops and renewal risks are identified only after invoice cancellation notices arrive.

---

## The Aperture Solution
An autonomous intelligence layer that deploys specialized AI agents to continuously monitor and execute operational workflows:
- **Autonomous Variance Engine**: Flags ARR discrepancies and missing contract line-items in real time.
- **Bi-Directional Ledger Sync**: Reconciles billing transactions against GAAP double-entry charts of accounts and syncs directly to Google Sheets and ERPs.
- **Continuous Predictive Forecasting**: Leverages historical customer consumption data to project next-quarter MRR within 2.5% accuracy.
